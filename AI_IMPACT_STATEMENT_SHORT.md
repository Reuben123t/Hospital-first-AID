# AI Impact Statement

Accredix is a hospital research prototype that helps organize brain MRI and
liver CT scan reviews. It can provide model results to support faster research
workflows, but it does not diagnose cancer or replace qualified clinicians.

Possible harms include false results, dataset bias, privacy risks, and
over-reliance on automated predictions. Use de-identified data, keep a human
in the loop, and complete clinical validation before any real patient use.
